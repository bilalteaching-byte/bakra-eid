Bakra Eid

Bakra Eid, also known as Eid al-Adha, is an important Islamic festival celebrated by Muslims worldwide. It commemorates the willingness of Prophet Ibrahim (Abraham) to sacrifice his son as an act of obedience to God. However, God provided a ram to sacrifice instead. During Bakra Eid, Muslims perform the ritual of Qurbani, where they sacrifice an animal (usually a goat, sheep, cow, or camel) and distribute the meat among family, friends, and those in need.

The festival is marked by special prayers, feasting, and charitable activities. It is a time for Muslims to come together, strengthen their faith, and share their blessings with others. The date of Bakra Eid varies each year based on the Islamic lunar calendar, and it is celebrated on the 10th day of Dhu al-Hijjah, the last month of the Islamic calendar.
